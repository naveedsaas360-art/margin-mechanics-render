## 9. Verify the channel
Go to **youtube.com/verify** and verify with your phone number. This unlocks:
- custom thumbnails (essential)
- videos longer than 15 minutes (our videos will be about 15 minutes)
- live and community features

## 10. Features
Studio → **Settings** → **Channel** → **Feature eligibility** → **Intermediate features**: make sure the status shows enabled after verifying.
