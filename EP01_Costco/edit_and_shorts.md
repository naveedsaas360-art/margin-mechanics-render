# EP01 — Assembly in Clipchamp (≈60–90 min, free, built into Windows)

Open: Start menu > type "Clipchamp" > Create a new video > 16:9.

## 1. Voice
Best: record script.md yourself on your phone (Voice Recorder app), quiet room, phone 20 cm from mouth.
Fallback: Clipchamp > Record & create > Text to speech > Voice "Andrew" or "Davis" (English US), speed 1.05x.
  Paste the script one section at a time (it has a length limit), so each section is its own audio clip.
  If you use text-to-speech, the "Altered or synthetic content" box must be YES when uploading.

## 2. Visuals
- Drag every PNG from /visuals onto the timeline at the [VISUAL] cues in script.md.
- Every still image: right-click > or use the "Pan & zoom" effect (Ken Burns) so nothing is static >4 sec.
- "Stock:" cues → Clipchamp > Content library > Videos (free filter ON), or pexels.com/videos. Keep each stock clip 3–5 sec.
- Rule: original charts on screen at least half the time. Don't swap them out for stock.

## 3. Music
- Clipchamp > Content library > Music > filter "Free" > genre "Corporate" or "Ambient". Volume 10–15%.
- Or YouTube Studio > Audio Library (zero copyright risk).

## 4. Text and pace
- Put a big number on screen whenever the narrator says one ($5.3B, 92.3%, $1.50).
- Cut every pause longer than 0.4 sec. Fast pacing = retention.

## 5. Export
Export > 1080p. Name it: EP01_Costco.mp4

## SHORT 1 — "Costco's real business"
Script: Costco sold almost $270 billion of products last year. But its membership fees alone — $5.3 billion — equal about two-thirds of its entire profit. Here's why: the warehouse is already built, the staff already paid. So nearly every dollar of your $65 fee goes straight to profit. The store is the bait. The membership is the business. Full breakdown on the channel.
Title: Costco's Real Business Isn't Groceries #shorts

## SHORT 2 — "The $1.50 hot dog"
Script: Costco's hot dog and soda has cost $1.50 since 1985. Adjusted for inflation it should cost several times that. So why won't they raise it? Because the hot dog isn't a product — it's a promise: we will never squeeze you. And that promise keeps over 90% of members renewing every single year. Full breakdown on the channel.
Title: Why Costco Will Never Raise the Hot Dog Price #shorts

## SHORT 3 — "The 14% rule"
Script: Costco is famous for capping its markups — around 14% on name brands, 15% on Kirkland. Supermarkets can go way higher. Why leave money on the table? Because if prices feel unbeatable, the membership pays for itself. And if it pays for itself, you renew. And renewals are where Costco's profit really lives. Full breakdown on the channel.
Title: Costco's Secret Pricing Rule #shorts
