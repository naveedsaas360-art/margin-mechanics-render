## Description
In a few days, chip stocks lost roughly a trillion dollars, and Intel had its worst day in decades. But the real story is where much of the AI money came from in the first place: chip makers invest in AI labs, AI labs rent computing from cloud companies, and cloud companies buy the chip makers' chips. Here's how the AI money loop works, why it made the boom look bigger, what debt has to do with it, and the 3 signals that tell you whether this ends in a soft landing or a real bust.
